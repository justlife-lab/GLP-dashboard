Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who this applies to

The GLP-1 Care CRM is an internal operations tool of Justlife Healthcare. It is used only by Justlife staff (who sign in with their Justlife Google Workspace account) and by authorised partner companies (who sign in with credentials issued by Justlife).

What Google sign-in shares with us

When a Justlife staff member signs in with Google, the app receives only your name, email address and Google Workspace domain. We use this solely to confirm that you are an authorised Justlife account and to record who made each change. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Client information

Client, treatment, refill and payment records are entered by authorised users and stored in Justlife's private Google Sheets database. They are used only to manage clients' GLP-1 treatment journeys and related operations. Each partner company can only see its own clients. All changes are recorded in an audit log.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties, except Google, which hosts the database and provides sign-in, and where required by law.

Security and retention

Access requires authentication. Permissions are enforced on the server and sessions expire automatically. Partner passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Records are kept as long as needed for treatment history and legal obligations.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Justlife Healthcare at sohaib.saqib@justlife.com.
